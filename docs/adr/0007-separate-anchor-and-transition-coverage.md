# Separate forward anchoring from transition coverage

A Research Revision qualifies as a Forward Research Anchor when its Current
Research State, Evidence closure, Anchor Coverage, point-in-time boundaries,
and market semantics can support future comparison. This qualification is
separate from both its historical research limitations and its Change
Conclusion. In particular, an Indeterminate Full Revision may automatically
establish a new Forward Research Anchor when it independently meets Anchor
Coverage; an update never skips the current chain head to reuse an older
anchor.

Research Cutoff remains a market-local date, while Information Frontier records
the actual point-in-time knowledge boundary for the Revision and each source.
Transition Coverage must prove continuity after the anchor's Information
Frontier through the update's Information Frontier. A disclosed archive gap
wholly before the anchor is an enduring historical limitation but does not
invalidate that forward transition. A gap after the anchor remains blocking for
authoritative No Material Change.

Anchor Coverage and Transition Coverage are expressed through source-independent
Market Research Capabilities whose market profiles identify acceptable or
complementary providers. Shadow may observe incomplete transitions because its
independent Full Analysis remains authoritative; an authoritative Incremental
Execution may not produce No Material Change without complete Transition
Coverage. United States and mainland-China equities remain Full-only until
their capability profiles and source-continuity contracts have been audited.
