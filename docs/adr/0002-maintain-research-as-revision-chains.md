# Maintain research as revision chains

Repeated analyses of the same Instrument will form a linear Research Chain
rather than remain independent runs. Each immutable Research Revision contains
the complete resulting Current Research State. Revision Role records whether it
establishes the initial state or updates a predecessor; Execution Strategy
records Full or Incremental work; only update Revisions have a separate Change
Conclusion of Material Change, No Material Change, or Indeterminate. Keeping
these axes separate avoids treating an initial analysis as a change conclusion
or a Full execution as necessarily material.

Reports and deliberation remain Research Artifacts, while persistent Claims,
Questions, scenarios, and Evidence relationships form the thesis that is
maintained over time. Prior Research identifies what must be reassessed but is
not Evidence for its own correctness. After independent Full research and state
assembly, a bounded Question Disposition step may map baseline Questions to
reaffirmed, answered, reopened, superseded, or retired states only with current
sealed Evidence. Omission never resolves a Question, and ambiguous mappings
preserve the old identity and expose limited coverage.

One Instrument may have alternative Research Chains, but only one is Primary.
Starting from an older Revision creates a new Research Fork instead of a second
head or merge obligation in the original chain. Preserving a linear identity
avoids rewriting history if alternative chains are used.

This structure costs more persistence work than linking reruns, but it directly
tests whether a live thesis is more useful than the project's existing
outcome-settlement memory. It also gives Incremental Execution a precise
baseline without treating an earlier conclusion as evidence.
