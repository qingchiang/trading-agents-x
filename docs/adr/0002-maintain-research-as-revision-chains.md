# Maintain research as revision chains

Repeated analyses of the same Instrument will form a linear Research Chain
rather than remain independent runs. Each immutable Research Revision contains
the complete resulting Current Research State and its change from the preceding
Revision. Reports and deliberation remain Research Artifacts, while persistent
Claims, Questions, scenarios, and Evidence relationships form the thesis that
is maintained over time. Prior Research identifies what must be reassessed but
is not Evidence for its own correctness.

One Instrument may have alternative Research Chains, but only one is Primary.
Starting from an older Revision creates a new Research Fork instead of a second
head or merge obligation in the original chain. Preserving a linear identity
avoids rewriting history if alternative chains are used.

This structure costs more persistence work than linking reruns, but it directly
tests whether a live thesis is more useful than the project's existing
outcome-settlement memory. It also gives Incremental Execution a precise
baseline without treating an earlier conclusion as evidence.
